# CU-11 Give calendar a usable narrow-screen presentation

**Priority:** P2 · **Evidence:** Source verified; browser follow-up required · **Baseline:** `25c7a864947994b8f321ceab73e6b8eae14e3107`

## Current behavior

Calendar always renders a seven-column month grid. Events are 20px high and truncated; day-add controls are hidden until hover. The month toolbar is a non-wrapping flex row, and the undated tray renders only the first 100 items without a continuation control.

## User impact and gap

On phones, narrow day cells and hidden add buttons make reading and scheduling difficult. A large undated backlog has a hard discovery boundary. Laptop calendar should retain spatial month context rather than being replaced everywhere.

## Reproduce

1. At 390px show a busy month with long titles and date ranges, then open the undated tray.
2. Repeat with 101 undated rows and inspect access to the final row.
3. At 1280px navigate month, open event and create on a chosen day using keyboard only.

## Likely ideal and proposed work

Offer agenda/day-list presentation on narrow screens with date headings and readable event targets. Preserve month mode on laptop and as an explicit option. Expose Add on touch/focus, provide a no-drag reschedule path and paginate/show all undated rows.

## Acceptance and reassessment

- [ ] All dated and undated rows remain reachable, including item 101.
- [ ] Primary event actions are readable and reachable at 320/390px.
- [ ] Ranges, undated records and the selected date property are understandable in both modes.

Run R11 in the [reassessment protocol](../../../../../docs/intent/collections-usability-audit-2026-11-11/reassessment.md). Record fixed commit, browser/device, before/after screenshots and test result. A source hypothesis becoming confirmed is a status/evidence update, not automatically a fix.
